Your Collection

Rules
  1. At the start, roll one each of a d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, and d20. Each roll is that fish's current size.
  2. Choose a fish in the pond, then roll a d6.
  3. If the total is equal to or higher than half the fish's size, rounded down, you catch it. The catch target is always at least 2.
  4. If the total is lower than the target, the fish swims away and is removed from play.
  5. A caught fish may be released: remember its current size, then reroll its original die until the new result is at least 1 larger. It returns to the pond at that larger size. A fish already at its die's maximum size cannot be released.
  6. Before rolling, you may choose one caught fish as chum. Its size is added to your d6 roll, and that fish is removed from your collection whether or not the cast succeeds.
  7. When no fish remain in the pond, you may still release caught fish to restock the pond and keep fishing. When you are ready to stop, choose Walk Away; the total size of the fish still in your collection is your ration reward.

The pond is quiet.
